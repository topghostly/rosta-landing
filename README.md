# Rosta

**Campaign management software for African influencer marketing agencies.**

Rosta helps agencies manage creators, track campaigns, impress clients, and understand profitability — all from one place.

---

## The Problem

Nigerian influencer agencies run multi-million naira campaigns on WhatsApp threads, Excel sheets, and manually built PowerPoint decks. Creators get missed. Profit gets lost. Clients get reports that look like a side hustle.

Global tools (AspireIQ, Modash, GRIN) charge $199–$2,350 a month in dollars, are built around US/EU markets, and don't understand how an agency in Lagos or Nairobi actually operates.

Rosta is built around how African agencies actually work.

## What Rosta Does

- **Campaign Workspace** — One table per campaign. Every creator, their status, their deliverable, their post link, and their financial contribution to your bottom line.
- **Profit Tracking** — See exactly what you charged the brand, what you paid the creator, and what you kept. Per creator. Per campaign. In naira.
- **Client Proposal Portal** — Share a branded link. The brand reviews creators, rates, and stats, then approves directly in their browser. No more PDFs over WhatsApp.
- **Creator Database** — Your agency's private, searchable database of every creator you've ever worked with. Owned by your agency, not by anyone's phone.
- **Status Pipeline** — Shortlisted → Briefed → Approved → Posted → Paid. Colour coded, overdue items auto-flagged, visible to your whole team.
- **One-Click Reports** — End of campaign, one click. A professional branded PDF report, shareable as a link or download.

## Who It's For

**Influencer marketing agencies in Nigeria and across Africa** — from solo operators running a handful of creators to teams managing dozens of campaigns a month.

- The buyer: agency founders, directors, and account managers
- The user: anyone on the agency team running campaigns day to day
- The audience: the brand clients you want to look professional in front of
